Delete your Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp data

When a business connects its accounts to MAIZ Agents, MAIZ holds a small amount of information from Meta. You can have all of it deleted at any time.

What MAIZ holds

  • The name and ID of the Facebook Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number you connected.
  • An access token that lets MAIZ act on that account only. It is stored encrypted.
  • The posts MAIZ drafted or published for you, and the likes, comments and follower figures MAIZ read for them.
  • For WhatsApp, the mobile number you chose to approve posts from, and the messages used to approve them.

How to delete it

  1. From MAIZ: sign in, open Social accounts and press Delete everything from Meta. It happens immediately.
  2. From Facebook: open Settings, then Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), find MAIZ Agents and press Remove. Choose to delete the information it received. Facebook then tells MAIZ, and MAIZ deletes the same information straight away and gives Facebook a confirmation code.
  3. By email: write to the address on our privacy policy and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deleting this data does not delete your MAIZ account, your team or your other business information. Billing records we are legally required to keep are not part of this data.
