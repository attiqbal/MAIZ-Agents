FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. You answer a few questions about your business, and your team starts working. Everything is described in plain language, like shopping online.

What does "energy" mean?

It is one weekly allowance your whole team shares, so a busy employee can use what a quiet one does not. Bigger tasks cost more, and you see the cost before a task starts. Redoing a task returns its energy. It refills every Monday, and adding an employee adds to it.

Will the AI send things without asking me?

By default, no. Anything that leaves your company, like a post, an email or a payment, waits for your approval, with a note on exactly what will be sent.

Can I trust the finance numbers?

The books are produced by deterministic, tested accounting code. The AI helps with matching receipts and explaining results, and every figure shows whether it is an estimate, something you told us, or verified from a document.

Which countries and languages are supported?

The product is global from day one with multi-currency, time zones and per-country rule packs. English is the only language at launch.

Is my business information safe?

Each business's data is kept apart from every other, your team only reads the apps you connect, and anything that would leave your company waits for your approval. The Security page lists what is in place today and what is still to come.

What if I need a role you do not have?

Describe the job to your Admin manager. It designs a specialist and drafts the job description, MAIZ checks it and confirms the price, and then you decide whether to hire it. Nothing is trained or charged before that.

What happens after the trial?

You pick a plan or you stop. We never ask for a card during the trial, so there is nothing to cancel.
