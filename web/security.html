In place today

Your data is kept apart

  • Every business has its own workspace. The database itself refuses to show one business another business's rows, even if our own code made a mistake.
  • This is checked by an automated test on every change.

Your books cannot be quietly changed

  • Accounting entries must balance before they are saved.
  • Entries are never edited or deleted. A mistake is fixed with a visible reversal.

You stay in control

  • Your AI employees write drafts. Nothing leaves your company on its own.
  • Roles decide who can do what: owner, admin, member or viewer.
  • Every action is written to an audit trail that cannot be rewritten.

Safe sign-in

  • No passwords to steal. You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email.
  • Links work once and expire in 15 minutes. Sessions end after 7 days.

Connections are protected

  • Slack access tokens are encrypted before they are stored.
  • Every message from Slack is checked to really come from Slack, and old copies are rejected.

Plain about the numbers

  • Finance figures come from tested accounting code, not from an AI guess.
  • Estimates are labelled as estimates.

Still to come

Your own database

  • Larger plans, or six months prepaid, are meant to get a separate database for your business. The plan is designed, but the separate databases are not switched on yet.

More defences for AI attacks

  • We are building tests and checks that stop hidden instructions in emails and documents from steering your employees.

Independent checks

  • We have not had an outside security audit or certification yet. We will say so here when we do.
