What we collect and why

We collect only what we need, and we tell you the lawful basis (UK GDPR) for each use:

  • Account details (name, work email, password in hashed form, business name, country, business type, sign-in and device records). To create and secure your account and run the service. Basis: contract.
  • Sign-in with Google or Microsoft (your verified email and name from the provider). To sign you in. Basis: contract.
  • Service content (what you tell your AI employees, tasks, documents and records you add or connect, approvals and the audit trail). To provide the service. Basis: contract. See “Data in your workspace” for personal data about other people.
  • Billing records (orders, plan, amounts, payment status; card details are handled by our payment provider once payments are switched on, and we do not store full card numbers). To take payment and keep tax records. Basis: contract and legal obligation.
  • Emails we send you (sign-in links, confirmations, account and approval notices). These are service messages. Basis: contract. We will only send you marketing with your consent or where the law allows, and you can opt out at any time.
  • Support messages and anything you send us. To help you and keep a record. Basis: legitimate interests.
  • Technical and security data (IP address, browser type, timestamps, errors, rate-limit and abuse records). To keep the service secure and working. Basis: legitimate interests.
  • Referral and promotion records (a code or link you arrived with, and what it unlocks). To credit the right promotion. Basis: legitimate interests.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of providers to run the service. They process data for us under contract and only on our instructions. We will keep this list up to date:

  • Hosting and infrastructure: Hetzner (servers and database) and Cloudflare (DNS and network), with encrypted backups kept off the server.
  • AI model providers: Anthropic (Claude) and RunPod (open models we run for you), which receive the text needed to carry out a task. They are not allowed to use it to train their models.
  • Email: Resend, to deliver our emails.
  • Sign-in providers: Google and Microsoft, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Connected apps: Slack, accounting, payments and other apps you connect, exchanging only what you allow.
  • Payments: our payment provider, once payments are switched on.
  • Professional advisers and authorities: our accountants, lawyers and insurers where needed, and the authorities where the law requires.

We do not sell personal data. If our business is sold or merged, the data may pass to the new owner, who must honour this policy.

Sending data outside the UK

Some providers, including AI providers, may process data outside the UK, for example in the United States or the European Economic Area. Where they do, we use a lawful transfer route, such as a UK adequacy decision, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses, with extra protections where needed. Ask us if you would like details.

Data in your workspace

Your workspace can hold personal data about your staff, customers and suppliers (for example in the HR desk or finance records). For that data you are the controller and we process it on your behalf under your instructions and the processing terms in our Terms of service. It is your job to have a lawful basis, tell those people, and add only what the task needs. Each business’s data is kept apart at the database level, and staff ID and document uploads are not accepted. If someone asks us about data in your workspace, we will pass the request to you.

How long we keep it

  • Account and workspace data: while your account is open, and for up to 30 days after it closes so you can export it, then deleted.
  • Billing and tax records: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as the law expects.
  • Audit trail entries: kept for the life of the account, because they are designed to be a permanent record, and removed with the workspace when it is deleted.
  • Security and technical logs: up to 12 months.
  • Backups: overwritten on a rolling cycle (up to 30 days), so deleted data can remain in a backup for that time and is not used.
  • Support messages: up to 3 years after they are closed.

How we protect it

Your data is kept apart by row-level database security, connections and stored app tokens are encrypted, sign-in uses one-time links or hashed passwords, and actions are written to an audit trail. See the Security page for what is in place today and what is not yet, including that we have not had an outside security audit. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts people at risk, we will tell you and, where required, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Your rights

You can ask for access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction, a portable copy, and to object to uses based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it. We will reply within one month. We may need to check who you are first. You also have the right not to be subject to a decision made only by automated means that has a legal or similarly significant effect; our AI employees draft and you decide, and nothing is approved or sent without you.

To use a right, write to %CONTACT_EMAIL%. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, and we would be glad to hear from you first.
