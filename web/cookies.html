The short version

We use what is needed to keep you signed in and the site working, plus one referral cookie that is set only if you open a referral link. We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. The operator is %COMPANY_NAME% (company number %COMPANY_NUMBER%); see our Privacy policy.

What the site uses

  • Sign-in and security cookies. When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, or connect an app, a short-lived cookie (maiz_login, maiz_oauth) proves the request came from your browser. They are set for a few minutes and then removed. Strictly necessary.
  • Referral cookie. If you arrive through a referral link (/r/…), a cookie (maiz_ref) remembers the code for 30 days so that the right person is credited when you sign up. It is set only when you open such a link. It is not strictly necessary for the site to work.
  • Browser storage. Your signed-in session, your set-up answers and your basket are kept in your browser’s local storage (keys starting maiz.) so you stay signed in and do not lose your place. It stays on your device until you sign out or clear it. Strictly necessary.
  • Fonts. The site loads its fonts (Fraunces and Inter) from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google, which sees your IP address. No cookie is set by us for this.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. If you block them, you will not be able to stay signed in.

If we later add anything else that is not strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it. The referral cookie above is the only optional one today; it is set only when you choose to open a referral link.
