FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

What does "energy" mean?

It is one weekly allowance your whole team shares, so a busy employee can use what a quiet one does not. Bigger tasks cost more, and you see the cost before a task starts. Redoing a task returns its energy. It refills every Monday, and adding an employee adds to it.

Which countries and languages are supported?

The product is global from day one with multi-currency, time zones and per-country rule packs. English is the only language at launch.

What happens after the trial?

You choose a plan or stop. We do not need a card during the trial.
