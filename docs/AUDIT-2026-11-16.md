# Website and setup audit, 16 November 2026

Every public page, the signup flow, every control centre screen (signed in as a new client, at 1440 and 375 wide) and the owner screens were opened and read. This is what was found and what was done about it in the same pull request. "Not built" items are listed at the end so nothing is hidden.

## Money: found and fixed

| Found | Fixed |
|---|---|
| **The £150 Team package could not be bought.** The only way to become a paying client was to add a £25 or £35 extra, which then started the whole paid plan with 8,000 energy a week. | The Team package is in the store, priced from the owner's Prices page. A trial client's first monthly order includes it (shown in the cart and on Stripe's page before paying). It cannot be bought twice. Clients who went paid before this keep their plan. See docs/STRIPE.md. |
| Cancelling a subscription changed nothing: the team and the paid plan stayed for ever. | Cancelling the Team package ends the plan at the end of the month paid for (or at once when Stripe ended it), then the team pauses like an ended trial, and buying again restarts it. |
| Discount codes (percent, pounds off, free months) were saved but never charged. | They come off the next monthly order at checkout and are sent to Stripe; the cart shows the line. See docs/PROMOTIONS.md. |
| No billing screen: no way to reach Stripe's page for card, invoices or cancelling, and no list of orders. | Settings has "Plan and billing": the plan in words, "Manage card, invoices and cancellation", and the orders with any code used. |
| Coming back from Stripe without paying showed nothing. | The store says "Payment cancelled. Nothing was charged, and your cart is still here." |
| Nothing told a trial client how to keep their team. | A "Choose your plan" button in the sidebar trial box, a chip on the Overview, and a Team package card at the top of the store. An ended trial or plan says so and says the work is kept. |

- `.env.example`, docs/PROMOTIONS.md and docs/STRIPE.md said payments or discounts were not built.

## The control centre: found and fixed

- **A real client saw made-up figures** on the Overview (cash £18,420, revenue £6,210, 412 customers, an invented briefing and activity feed, "62% of 8,000 used") under an "Example" tag. A signed-in client now sees their own energy shares and their own latest work, and where a figure needs a connected app the panel says so and links to connecting it.
- Energy and spend showed "Energy left today 300 / 300" per employee although the team shares one pool, and four invented "biggest tasks". Now each employee's share of the week and the client's real biggest tasks.
- The sidebar was taller than a laptop screen (24 entries): Energy, the store, First day and Settings were below the fold. Six of the entries were filters of the same Apps screen. Now 16 entries in three groups that fit a 900px-high screen; Slack and Social accounts are cards at the top of Apps and connections; hiring sits with the team.
- The Apps screen was 26,000 pixels tall (281 app cards). It now opens on the 12 apps that connect in one step, with search, type filters and "Show all 281 apps".
- The "Connect through Zapier or Make" button overflowed its card. Shorter label, and buttons in app cards wrap.
- "Free trial until ..." was a pop-up that covered the Overview tiles on a phone. Replaced by the chip and the sidebar box.
- The top bar showed "Wallet £0.00", which meant nothing to a client. It now shows the energy left.
- The "When do you need it?" row was not lined up with the page.
- A single "Needs you" card sat in a corner of an empty panel. It now takes more of the row.
- The notification bell could only alert while the page was open. It now offers real alerts on the device.
- The trial box at the foot of the sidebar was cut off on a 900px-high screen, so "Choose your plan" could not be seen without scrolling the sidebar. The box is now two lines (days left and the button), the sidebar spacing is a little tighter so all 16 entries and the box fit, and on a shorter screen the box stays in view while the list scrolls above it.
- Energy and spend said "at most 3,000 a day" to a trial client whose whole trial is 3,000. The daily figure now shows only when it is less than the week's energy.
- "People today" on the Overview said "Records complete 100%" for a client with no staff. It now says there are no staff records yet.
- The store said extra energy for a consultant pass is "taken from your wallet". There is no wallet. It now says it is never more than the cap the client set.

## Found by the code review and the security review, and fixed

- A website built to be slow to read could stall the whole server from the signup page, with no login. The reader now looks only near the top of a page and finds tags by position; pages built to be slow are read in a few milliseconds (tested).
- Inside addresses written as IPv6 (for example `::ffff:7f00:1`) were treated as public by the check that protects the website reader and outgoing webhooks. Fixed and tested.
- After paying on Stripe the Team package stayed in the saved cart, where it could not be removed and blocked every later checkout. The cart is emptied on return, anything the client cannot buy leaves it, and the package is no longer offered to a client who has it.
- Two open checkouts could both be paid for the Team package. A new one now closes the older unpaid one.
- A client who cancelled and bought again lost top-up energy they had paid for. It is kept.
- A cancelled plan was called "your free trial" in chat and in the owner's client list, and trial codes could extend it. It now has its own words and status.
- A month paid for on the 31st ended a few days into the month after next. It now ends on the shorter month's last day.
- One browser used by two people kept sending the first person's alerts. The browser now belongs to whoever logged in last, logging out turns alerts off, and someone who has left a team is not told about its work.
- An outside tool sending more than 200 jobs in a day could push its own jobs out of the record and win back its daily energy limit. Jobs from the last day are always kept.
- One client could use up a shared daily search budget (YouTube, X, share prices) for every client with searches that cost them nothing. Each client now has a share.
- Keyboard focus was lost after pressing Share, Remove, Pause or Approve; a failed action left its button disabled. Both fixed.

## Found, not built in this round

- Extras bought after the Team package are separate monthly orders, and cancelling the package does not cancel them. The client is told and can cancel each one on Stripe's page.
- The check that an address is public resolves its name once and the request resolves it again, so a hostile name server could change its answer in between. The request is https to port 443 with a certificate check, which limits what this can reach; pinning the address is not built.

- Removing an add-on employee when its own monthly order is cancelled (Stripe stops charging; the employee stays until this is added).
- Approvals, Books and cash and the Compliance calendar still show labelled example data until those features have real data behind them.
- Inviting teammates to a client's workspace.
- The Admin manager's planning interview for outside agents (stage 2 of docs/EXTERNAL-AGENTS.md).
