# The MAIZ Office: campaign plan

Plan for a weekly comedy series on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Read first: [00-positioning.md](00-positioning.md) (what we say and never say) and [01-cast-bible.md](01-cast-bible.md) (the characters). Scripts are in `10-` to `14-`. How the videos are made and approved is in [03-production-workflow.md](03-production-workflow.md).

## 1. Goal and principles

- Goal: make owner-run small businesses understand in 20 seconds that MAIZ is a team, that they stay in control, and that it costs one known price, then start the free trial.
- 140 scripts: 120 ready to schedule now (24 weeks, five a week) and 20 gated scripts that ship only after the matching feature is built and checked (section 8).
- One joke and one product point per video. Warm, dry, never mean. No fake testimonials and no claim that the product cannot back (see positioning section 7).
- Every post carries a call to action and a tracked link. Nothing is posted without Atif's sign-off (section 9 of the workflow doc).

## 4. Platforms and rhythm

- Post the same vertical file on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, Monday to Friday, one video a day. Facebook Reels and LinkedIn are optional extra copies for the same file (owners are on both); keep the same caption but check each platform's captions limit.
- Starting times (UK): Reels 12:30, Shorts 17:00, TikTok 18:00. Test alternative windows in weeks 5 and 6 and keep the best per platform.
- Post at the same time each day so the audience forms a habit; hero episodes (45s) go on Fridays.
- Each account profile: one line ("A comedy about an AI back-office team. Free trial: no card."), the link, and the disclosure that the characters are fictional and made with AI.
- Reply to comments for the first hour; answer questions with the site's real answers only; never promise features that are not live.

## 6. Calls to action, links and codes

- The same CTA family every time: "Start free. 7 days. No card." plus the character's line where it fits.
- **Tracked link** (one per post): `https://maizagents.com/?utm_source={tiktok|instagram|youtube|facebook|linkedin}&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=maiz-office&utm_content={SCRIPT-ID}&utm_term={character}`. This works with the analytics already described in `docs/GROWTH-ANALYTICS.md` and needs no new build. Each platform's bio link is the same with `utm_content=bio`.
- **Optional character codes** (decision for Atif): FELIX, POPPY, BASIL, HANA, CATO, MAYA, as promo codes created in the owner promotions page (see `docs/PROMOTIONS.md`). Default is tracking only: the codes give no extra discount, the CTA never says "discount", and the trial offer stays as on the site. If you want a real offer (for example extra trial days), set it in owner promotions first, then the scripts can name it. Nothing in any script says a discount unless the code is live.
- **Attribution report:** weekly, trials and paid starts by `utm_content` (script), `utm_term` (character) and source (platform), so each character's code or link can be compared.

## 7. Metrics and how we double down on winners

Starting measures per video (platform analytics): 3-second view rate (hook), average percentage watched, completion, shares and saves per 1,000 views, follows, link clicks. Down the funnel from the tracked link: visits, trial starts, paid starts. Targets are not guessed here: weeks 1 to 4 set our own baseline, then the goal is to beat our own top quartile.

Weekly review (Monday, 30 minutes, Atif or whoever owns it):

1. Rank the last week's videos by completion times shares.
2. Rank by trial starts per 1,000 views from the tracked link.
3. **Double down rule:** for any video in the top 20% on either rank, make three follow-ups within two weeks: (a) same joke with a new hook, (b) same product beat with a different character, (c) a 40-second version with more story. Re-cut the original with a new caption and repost after 60 days.
4. **Stop rule:** retire a hook style, character or pillar that is in the bottom 20% for three weeks running; keep one in five slots as a trial of something new.
5. **Boost rule:** put a small paid boost behind the top 10% only. Budget is Atif's decision; no budget is assumed here.
6. Write the finding in the log: "what we tried, what happened, what we do next".

Also track comments that ask questions and feed the real answers into the FAQ and into new scripts.
