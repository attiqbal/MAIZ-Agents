# Scripts J to L (30 videos): Handoffs, Grow and connect, After hours and seasons

Same format and rules as [10-scripts-A-C.md](10-scripts-A-C.md). New recurring character used in pillar L: **Ollie**, the nervous human intern (added to the cast bible, section 4b). Ollie is a person, not an AI; the joke is that the AI team makes Ollie's day easier, not that it replaces Ollie.

## Pillar J: Passive-aggressive handoffs (J01 to J10)

Pillar J · Maya, Poppy · 15s · Feature: briefing, routing (live)

- **Dialogue:** POPPY: "What's happening Friday?" MAYA: "As per my briefing." POPPY: "...Friday's lovely."

- **CTA:** "Meet Maya."
