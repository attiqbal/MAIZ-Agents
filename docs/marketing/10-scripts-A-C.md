# Scripts A to C (30 videos): Monday done, Your yes, Money you can trace

Read [01-cast-bible.md](01-cast-bible.md) and [00-positioning.md](00-positioning.md) first. Format of every script:

- **ID · Title**, then pillar, characters, length, the product feature shown (all stated on the live site; "verify" = check in the running product before filming).
- **Shots**: numbered, one setting (the office set), max six.
- **Dialogue**: spoken lines. `BOSS` is off-screen voice or a Slack bubble.
- **On-screen text** (captions and cards), **CTA**, and **Hook** (first two seconds).
- Every video also carries the standard disclosure card, the logo stamp and the tracked link (see [02-campaign-plan.md](02-campaign-plan.md)). Insert screens are always labelled "Example".
