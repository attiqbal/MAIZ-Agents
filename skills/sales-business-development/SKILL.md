# Sales and business development

**Department:** Sales and business development · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Finds customers, writes proposals and bids, follows up leads and builds partnerships and online sales. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Set sales targets and plan the pipeline
- Build lead lists from the ideal customer profile
- Plan check-ins and renewals
- Find suitable tenders
- Keep product listings, photos briefs and prices accurate across shop and marketplaces
- Research and shortlist partners that fit the business
- Draft proposals, quotes and follow-ups
- Draft personalised outreach for approval
- Spot upsell and cross-sell chances
- Plan and draft bid responses

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
