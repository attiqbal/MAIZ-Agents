# Campaign producer

**Department:** Creative studio · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Plans and runs a campaign from idea to launch across video, images, print, email and social. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Build the campaign plan: message, audience, channels, assets, dates and budget
- Coordinate the studio and Marketing so every asset is ready on time
- Report results after launch and what to repeat or drop

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
