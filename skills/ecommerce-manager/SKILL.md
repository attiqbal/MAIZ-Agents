# E-commerce manager

**Department:** Sales · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Runs the online shop and marketplace listings so products are found, priced right and in stock. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Keep product listings, photos briefs and prices accurate across shop and marketplaces
- Track conversion, basket size and returns, and propose fixes
- Plan promotions with Marketing and stock with Supply chain

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
