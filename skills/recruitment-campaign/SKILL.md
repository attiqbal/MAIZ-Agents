# Recruitment campaign runner

**Department:** Task hire · People and operations · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Runs the paperwork side of hiring for one role, from job ad to shortlist. Hired for one task at a fixed price, not as a permanent team member.

## Engagement: task hire

**Fixed price:** £129 · **Included energy:** 3000 · **Turnaround:** 5 to 10 working days

**Covered by the fixed price:**

- Job description and ad
- Screening questions and scorecard
- Shortlist from up to 50 applications you share

**Not included:**

- Job board fees
- Interviewing candidates

**Approval points:**

- Job ad
- Shortlist

**If the included energy runs out:**

- Your Admin manager tells you when the task has used 80% of its included energy.
- When it is all used, work pauses. Nothing extra is charged until you choose: approve more energy up to a cap you set, cut the scope, or stop and keep what is done.
- Approved extra energy costs £1 per 100 energy, taken from your wallet.
- Redoing work because the employee got it wrong never counts against the allowance.

## Works with

- Admin manager (hires, briefs, tracks the allowance and collects approvals)
- Any team member whose work this task feeds
