# Import and export coordinator

**Department:** Supply chain and procurement · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Prepares the paperwork for goods crossing borders so shipments are not held or over-taxed. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Prepare commercial invoices, packing lists and customs information for the broker
- Check commodity codes, origin and duty with dated official sources
- Track shipments through customs and record costs

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
