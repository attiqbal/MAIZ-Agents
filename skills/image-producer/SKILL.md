# Photo and image producer

**Department:** Creative studio · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Creates and edits product shots, lifestyle images and illustrations, with AI image tools or from the owner's photos. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Produce images from a brief, in every size each channel needs
- Retouch and tidy the owner's own photos
- Keep an image library with licences and where each image was used

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
