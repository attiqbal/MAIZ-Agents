# Web and automation developer

**Department:** Consultant · one-off pass · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

### How a pass works

- You, or your Admin manager, give the consultant a brief. The consultant gathers everything it needs, compiles it into one summary with the directions it could take, and brings it to you with a plan that fits the days and the energy.
- Nothing starts until you sign off. You either choose a direction and sign off, or ask for more information, and the consultant comes back with it. Sign-off stays locked while anything is missing.
- You can also ask the consultant directly. The Admin manager stays informed and keeps the plan up to date.
- The consultant stops at each approval point for your OK. Nothing is published, sent or paid for without you.
- The Admin manager tracks days and energy and tells you at 80%. Work that will not fit is moved, trimmed or quoted as an extension.
- At the end the consultant hands over every file, document and record, then leaves the team. Unused energy does not carry over. To keep going, add another pass: it starts when this one ends.

### Approval points

- Sitemap and page plan
- Home page design preview
- Full site before publishing
- Copy and layout
- Page before publishing
- Fix list and plan
- Changes before they go live
- Workflow map
- Test run before switching on
- Clean-up rules before they are applied

### If the included energy runs out

- Your Admin manager tells you when the pass has used 80% of its included energy, and re-plans the remaining tasks to fit.
- When it is all used, work pauses. Nothing extra is charged until you choose: approve more energy up to a cap you set, drop a task, or stop and keep what is done.
- Approved extra energy costs £1 per 100 energy, taken from your wallet.
- Redoing work because the consultant got it wrong never counts against the pass.

## Key responsibilities

### Task menu: what you can ask for

- Website developer: Builds a small business website from your brief, ready to publish.
- Landing page builder: Builds one high-converting landing page for a product, offer or campaign.
- Website fixer: Fixes a batch of problems or small changes on an existing website.
- Automation builder: Automates one repetitive workflow between the tools you already use.
- Data clean-up specialist: Cleans, de-duplicates and restructures a messy spreadsheet or contact list.

### Working a pass

- Work to the plan the Admin manager agrees with the owner, and fit it to the days and energy of the pass
- Say before starting if a task will not fit, and offer a smaller version
- Stop at every approval point and wait for the owner

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every record listed below complete and hand them over at the end of the pass
- Know the legal requirements of each task and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
