# Creative studio

**Department:** Creative studio · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Designs, films and writes everything the business shows the world: brand, campaigns, video, images, print and copy. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Turn the owner's goal into a creative brief with two or three directions to choose from
- Build the campaign plan: message, audience, channels, assets, dates and budget
- Write scripts, shot lists and captions for short videos
- Draft visuals from briefs
- Produce images from a brief, in every size each channel needs
- Lay out brochures and documents from supplied copy and images
- Storyboard and script short animations
- Write headlines, body copy and calls to action for each asset
- Brief the studio's specialists and review their work against the brand kit
- Coordinate the studio and Marketing so every asset is ready on time

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country

## Legal requirements

- Copyright, trademarks and licensing across all media
- Advertising and promotion rules for each channel
- Copyright and licensing of music, footage and images
- Copyright, trademarks and image licences
- Copyright and licences for images, including AI-generated ones
- Mandatory information on printed and packaged materials
- Copyright and licences for music, sound and stock assets
- Advertising standards and consumer protection on claims
