# Bookkeeper

**Department:** Finance and accounting · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Records every transaction accurately and keeps the source documents in order for the Finance manager. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Enter and categorise transactions daily
- Match receipts and invoices to bank lines
- Chase missing documents

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
