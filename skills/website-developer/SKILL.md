# Website developer

**Department:** Task hire · Web and technology · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Builds a small business website from your brief, ready to publish. Hired for one task at a fixed price, not as a permanent team member.

## Engagement: task hire

**Fixed price:** £249 · **Included energy:** 6000 · **Turnaround:** 5 to 10 working days

**Covered by the fixed price:**

- Up to 5 pages (for example home, about, services, pricing, contact)
- Mobile-friendly design in your brand colours
- Contact form, basic SEO and analytics set-up
- Two rounds of changes

**Not included:**

- Online shop or bookings system
- Logo design
- Domain and hosting fees
- Copywriting beyond the page text from your brief

**Approval points:**

- Sitemap and page plan
- Home page design preview
- Full site before publishing

**If the included energy runs out:**

- Your Admin manager tells you when the task has used 80% of its included energy.
- When it is all used, work pauses. Nothing extra is charged until you choose: approve more energy up to a cap you set, cut the scope, or stop and keep what is done.
- Approved extra energy costs £1 per 100 energy, taken from your wallet.
- Redoing work because the employee got it wrong never counts against the allowance.
