# Social media and content

**Department:** Social media and content · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Keeps the business visible online: posts, articles, search, ads, newsletters and CRM follow-up. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Plan and draft posts for each platform
- Draft articles and newsletters from the content plan
- Keyword research and content briefs
- Plan campaigns and audiences
- Segment the customer list and keep consent records current
- Draft replies to comments and messages
- Rewrite and update old content
- On-page and technical SEO checks
- Write ad variants for testing
- Plan and draft email journeys: welcome, win-back, renewal

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
