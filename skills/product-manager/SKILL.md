# Product manager

**Department:** Product and research · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Decides what the product should do next and why. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Gather customer needs and feedback
- Write product briefs and roadmaps
- Measure what launched

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
