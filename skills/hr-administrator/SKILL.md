# HR administrator

**Department:** People (HR) · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Keeps staff records, contracts and absence tracking accurate and up to date. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Keep staff files, contracts and right-to-work checks complete
- Track holidays, sickness and probation dates and remind managers
- Prepare letters and forms for the HR manager to check
