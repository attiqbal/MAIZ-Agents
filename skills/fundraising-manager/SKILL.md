# Fundraising and investor relations manager

**Department:** Finance and accounting · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Prepares the business to raise money and keeps lenders, investors and grant bodies informed. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
