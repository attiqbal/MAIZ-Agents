# Management accountant

**Department:** Finance and accounting · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Turns the books into monthly management accounts, budgets and cost reports the owner can steer by. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
