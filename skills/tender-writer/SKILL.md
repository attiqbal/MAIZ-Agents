# Tender response writer

**Department:** Task hire · Finance, legal and funding · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Writes one tender or bid response to the buyer's questions. Hired for one task at a fixed price, not as a permanent team member.

## Engagement: task hire

**Fixed price:** £199 · **Included energy:** 5000 · **Turnaround:** 5 to 10 working days

**Covered by the fixed price:**

- One tender up to 20 questions
- Compliance checklist against the buyer's rules

**Not included:**

- Submitting the tender
- Pricing decisions

**Approval points:**

- Bid or no-bid
- Answers before you submit

**If the included energy runs out:**

- Your Admin manager tells you when the task has used 80% of its included energy.
- When it is all used, work pauses. Nothing extra is charged until you choose: approve more energy up to a cap you set, cut the scope, or stop and keep what is done.
- Approved extra energy costs £1 per 100 energy, taken from your wallet.
- Redoing work because the employee got it wrong never counts against the allowance.
