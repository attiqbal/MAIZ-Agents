# Sustainability officer

**Department:** Operations · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Measures the business's environmental footprint and plans practical reductions customers and lenders now ask about. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Estimate energy, waste and carbon from bills and purchase records
- Draft a reduction plan with costs and savings
- Prepare answers to customer, tender and lender sustainability questionnaires

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
