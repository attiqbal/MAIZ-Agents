# Staff handbook writer

**Department:** Task hire · People and operations · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Drafts a staff handbook and core HR policies for your country. Hired for one task at a fixed price, not as a permanent team member.

## Engagement: task hire

**Fixed price:** £149 · **Included energy:** 3500 · **Turnaround:** 4 to 6 working days

**Covered by the fixed price:**

- Handbook with up to 12 policies
- Tailored to your country, size and way of working

**Not included:**

- Employment-law advice or a lawyer's sign-off

**Approval points:**

- Policy list
- Draft handbook

**If the included energy runs out:**

- Your Admin manager tells you when the task has used 80% of its included energy.
- When it is all used, work pauses. Nothing extra is charged until you choose: approve more energy up to a cap you set, cut the scope, or stop and keep what is done.
- Approved extra energy costs £1 per 100 energy, taken from your wallet.
- Redoing work because the employee got it wrong never counts against the allowance.
