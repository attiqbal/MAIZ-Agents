# Legal and contracts

**Department:** Legal and contracts · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Keeps contracts, policies, licences, data protection, health and safety and insurance in order. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Keep a contract register with key terms and dates
- Keep the record of processing activities
- Draft risk assessments
- List the licences and permits the business needs, with the issuing body
- Keep a risk register with owners, likelihood and impact
- Compare drafts against the business's standard terms and flag risks
- Draft privacy notices and impact assessments
- Plan inspections, drills and training
- Prepare applications and renewals for the owner to sign and submit
- Compare cover against the risks and list gaps for the broker

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country

## Legal requirements

- Contract law basics of the owner's country
- GDPR-style data protection law and breach reporting deadlines
- Workplace health and safety law
- Licensing law for the business's sector and location
- Compulsory insurance for the business's country and sector
- When a lawyer must review
- International data transfer rules
- Accident reporting duties
