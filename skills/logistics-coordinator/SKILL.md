# Logistics coordinator

**Department:** Supply chain and procurement · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Gets goods delivered on time and with the right paperwork. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
