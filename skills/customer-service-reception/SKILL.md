# Customer service and reception

**Department:** Customer service and reception · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Answers calls, enquiries, chats and complaints, keeps bookings in order and greets visitors. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Answer phone, email and web enquiries and route them to the right person
- Draft answers to emails and chats
- Plan onboarding for new customers
- Answer booking requests and confirm appointments from the calendar
- Draft replies to reviews and comments for approval
- Take messages and callback requests and make sure they are followed up
- Keep the help articles up to date
- Track health scores and risks
- Send reminders and manage cancellations, no-shows and the waiting list
- Flag complaints and risks to Customer support the same day

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
