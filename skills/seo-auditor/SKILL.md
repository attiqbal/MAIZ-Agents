# SEO auditor

**Department:** Task hire · Marketing and research · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Audits your website's search performance and gives a fix list in priority order. Hired for one task at a fixed price, not as a permanent team member.

## Engagement: task hire

**Fixed price:** £79 · **Included energy:** 2000 · **Turnaround:** 2 to 4 working days

**Covered by the fixed price:**

- Technical, on-page and content audit of up to 50 pages
- Keyword gap list
- Prioritised fix plan

**Not included:**

- Making the fixes (see Website fixer)
- Link building

**Approval points:**

- Final report

**If the included energy runs out:**

- Your Admin manager tells you when the task has used 80% of its included energy.
- When it is all used, work pauses. Nothing extra is charged until you choose: approve more energy up to a cap you set, cut the scope, or stop and keep what is done.
- Approved extra energy costs £1 per 100 energy, taken from your wallet.
- Redoing work because the employee got it wrong never counts against the allowance.
