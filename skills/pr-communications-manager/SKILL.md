# PR and communications manager

**Department:** PR and communications · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Gets the business fair attention in the press and keeps its public statements consistent. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
