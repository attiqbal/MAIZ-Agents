# Executive and office support

**Department:** Executive and office support · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Runs the owner's diary, inbox and travel, keeps the office running and handles hiring and staff admin. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Draft replies and triage the inbox into act, delegate and file
- Manage office suppliers, leases and services
- Write and post job ads
- Run training-needs analysis
- Keep staff files, contracts and right-to-work checks complete
- Plan meetings, agendas and minutes
- Keep asset and equipment lists
- Screen applications against the scorecard
- Plan and schedule training
- Track holidays, sickness and probation dates and remind managers

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
