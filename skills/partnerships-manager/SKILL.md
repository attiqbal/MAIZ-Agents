# Partnerships manager

**Department:** Sales · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Finds and looks after resellers, affiliates and partners who bring in customers. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Research and shortlist partners that fit the business
- Draft partner proposals and terms for the owner to send
- Track each partner's results and renewals

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
