# Payroll specialist

**Department:** Finance and accounting · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Prepares accurate, on-time payroll and the records and filings payroll law requires. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Prepare payroll from hours, salaries and changes
- Prepare pay slips and payroll filings for approval
- Keep the payroll calendar

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
