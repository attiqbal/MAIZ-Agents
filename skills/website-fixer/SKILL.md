# Website fixer

**Department:** Task hire · Web and technology · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Fixes a batch of problems or small changes on an existing website. Hired for one task at a fixed price, not as a permanent team member.

## Engagement: task hire

**Fixed price:** £49 · **Included energy:** 1000 · **Turnaround:** 1 to 3 working days

**Covered by the fixed price:**

- Up to 10 small fixes or changes from one list
- Speed and broken-link check
- Before and after notes

**Not included:**

- Redesigns or new pages
- Work on sites we cannot access safely

**Approval points:**

- Fix list and plan
- Changes before they go live

**If the included energy runs out:**

- Your Admin manager tells you when the task has used 80% of its included energy.
- When it is all used, work pauses. Nothing extra is charged until you choose: approve more energy up to a cap you set, cut the scope, or stop and keep what is done.
- Approved extra energy costs £1 per 100 energy, taken from your wallet.
- Redoing work because the employee got it wrong never counts against the allowance.
