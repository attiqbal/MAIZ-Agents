# Risk and insurance coordinator

**Department:** Legal, risk and compliance · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Keeps a simple risk register and makes sure the business is insured for what could really hurt it. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Keep a risk register with owners, likelihood and impact
- Compare cover against the risks and list gaps for the broker
- Prepare renewal information and track claims

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
