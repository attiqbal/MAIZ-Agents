# Rota and shift coordinator

**Department:** People (HR) · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Builds fair staff rotas that cover demand and respect working-time rules. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
