# Creative director

**Department:** Creative studio · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Leads the studio: turns a business goal into a creative idea and keeps every piece of media on brand. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Turn the owner's goal into a creative brief with two or three directions to choose from
- Brief the studio's specialists and review their work against the brand kit
- Present finished work to the owner for approval, with what was changed and why

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
