# Customer support agent

**Department:** Customer service · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Answers customer questions in the business's voice and hands over when unsure. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Draft answers to emails and chats
- Keep the help articles up to date
- Escalate complaints and refunds to the owner
