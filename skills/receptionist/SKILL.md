# Receptionist

**Department:** Customer service and reception · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Is the friendly front desk: answers calls and enquiries, greets visitors and passes people to the right person. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Answer phone, email and web enquiries and route them to the right person
- Take messages and callback requests and make sure they are followed up
- Keep visitor and appointment lists and prepare the front desk day sheet

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
