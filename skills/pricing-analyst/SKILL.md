# Pricing analyst

**Department:** Product and research · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Works out what to charge, from costs, competitors and what customers will pay. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Build a price and margin model for each product or service
- Track competitor prices from public sources
- Propose and review price tests with the owner

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
