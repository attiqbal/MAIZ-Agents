# Brand kit designer

**Department:** Task hire · Design and content · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Creates a simple brand kit: logo ideas, colours, fonts and how to use them. Hired for one task at a fixed price, not as a permanent team member.

## Engagement: task hire

**Fixed price:** £99 · **Included energy:** 2000 · **Turnaround:** 3 to 5 working days

**Covered by the fixed price:**

- 3 logo concepts, 1 refined
- Colour palette and font pairing
- One-page brand guide
- Two rounds of changes

**Not included:**

- Trademark registration
- Printed materials

**Approval points:**

- Concept choice
- Final kit

**If the included energy runs out:**

- Your Admin manager tells you when the task has used 80% of its included energy.
- When it is all used, work pauses. Nothing extra is charged until you choose: approve more energy up to a cap you set, cut the scope, or stop and keep what is done.
- Approved extra energy costs £1 per 100 energy, taken from your wallet.
- Redoing work because the employee got it wrong never counts against the allowance.
