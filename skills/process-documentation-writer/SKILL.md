# Process and knowledge writer

**Department:** Operations · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Writes down how the business works so anyone, human or AI, can do a job the same way. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Interview the owner and team and write step-by-step procedures
- Keep a knowledge base organised, dated and easy to search
- Review procedures on a schedule and after every change

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
