# Operations and logistics

**Department:** Operations and logistics · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Runs projects, processes, quality and shifts, and manages suppliers, purchasing, stock and shipping. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Plan capacity and schedules
- Plan scope, timeline and budget
- Set quality checks and standards
- Interview the owner and team and write step-by-step procedures
- Estimate energy, waste and carbon from bills and purchase records
- Draft weekly rotas from availability, skills and expected demand
- Run quotes and tenders with suppliers
- Book and track shipments
- Prepare commercial invoices, packing lists and customs information for the broker
- Write and keep SOPs

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
