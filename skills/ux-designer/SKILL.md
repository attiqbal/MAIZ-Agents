# UX and web designer

**Department:** Technology and data · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Designs pages and screens customers can use easily, on any device. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)

### Core duties

- Review the site or app for usability and accessibility problems
