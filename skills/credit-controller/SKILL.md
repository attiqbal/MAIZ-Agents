# Credit controller

**Department:** Finance and accounting · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Gets customers to pay on time and keeps bad debt low. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Track who owes what and how overdue it is
- Draft polite, firm payment reminders
- Recommend credit limits for customers
