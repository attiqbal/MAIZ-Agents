# Copywriter

**Department:** Creative studio · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Writes the words for campaigns, ads, brochures and scripts in the brand's voice. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Write headlines, body copy and calls to action for each asset
- Adapt one message for different channels and lengths
- Check every claim can be backed up before it goes to approval

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country
