# Product and research

**Department:** Product and research · **Reports to:** Owner

## Role summary

Researches markets, competitors and prices and plans what to build and sell next. Trained as a custom specialist for this business from the role blueprint and the owner's brief.

## Works with

- Admin manager (routing, training and check-ins)
- Any teammate whose job description covers related work

## Key responsibilities

### Core duties

- Gather customer needs and feedback
- Research questions from the team
- Market sizing and segmentation
- Build a price and margin model for each product or service
- Write product briefs and roadmaps
- Summarise findings with sources
- Competitor tracking
- Track competitor prices from public sources

### Documentation and legal records

- Keep every document and record listed below complete and up to date
- Know the legal requirements of the job and say when a rule must be confirmed for the owner's country

## Legal requirements

- Product safety and labelling where physical
- Copyright and fair use of sources
- Competition law on sharing information
- Competition law: no price-fixing or sharing prices with competitors
- Accessibility and consumer rules for digital products
- Data protection when research involves people
- Copyright of reports used
- Price display and promotion rules
